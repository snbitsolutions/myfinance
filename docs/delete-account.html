Request account deletion

ADEPA TEACHERS’ CO-OPERATIVE CREDIT UNION LTD

This page explains how to request deletion of your ADEPA TEACHERS mobile app account when you cannot use the app or have already uninstalled it. Uninstalling the app alone does not close your account or delete information held by ADEPA.

How to submit a request

Email swedruteachers@yahoo.com with the subject ADEPA TEACHERS account deletion request.

Alternatively, call +233596921255 for assistance with your request.

Include your name and the phone number or email address registered to your app account so ADEPA can identify the account. ADEPA may contact you to verify your identity before acting on the request.

Do not email your password, PIN, one-time code, or other sign-in credentials.

What happens to your information

After your identity and request are verified, ADEPA will review and process the account-deletion request. Personal profile and app-account information that is no longer needed will be deleted or de-identified where permitted. ADEPA may retain member, account, transaction, loan, payment, audit, or related records when required for financial recordkeeping, legal or regulatory compliance, dispute resolution, or security.

Required records are retained only for the applicable obligation or retention period. The period depends on the record type and legal or regulatory requirements; ADEPA can explain what applies to your request. ADEPA will inform you of the outcome and any information that must be retained.

Need help?

Contact ADEPA Teachers’ Co-operative Credit Union Ltd by telephone at +233596921255 or email at swedruteachers@yahoo.com. See the Privacy Policy for further information about how ADEPA handles personal information.
